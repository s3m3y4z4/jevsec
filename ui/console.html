Engagement console

Everything on this page reads and writes a local session. The tool sorts and explains; you decide.

  1. Open a session — name it after the engagement.

    top right: type a name, press open

    Sessions persist on disk; closing this page loses nothing.

  2. Send material — scanner findings as JSON, and what you saw as observations tied to an objective.

    right column: Findings / Observations tabs, one JSON object per line

  3. Read the queue — every record shows its verdict or impact, the probability of each judgment, and a suggested next step. Open a record to see the redacted text it was judged on, and to leave feedback.

    Below any confidence threshold, or on any error, the record is marked for review: nothing is auto-approved.
